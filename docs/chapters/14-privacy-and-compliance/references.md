# References: Privacy, Compliance, and Organizational Context

1. [Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Educational_Rights_and_Privacy_Act) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive overview of the US student-privacy law that constrains K-12 and higher-ed xAPI deployments, including the school-official exception.

2. [Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children%27s_Online_Privacy_Protection_Act) - Wikipedia - The under-13 privacy regulation in the US, with the parental-consent and verifiable-consent requirements that shape K-12 actor identification.

3. [General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Data_Protection_Regulation) - Wikipedia - Detailed coverage of the EU's data-protection regulation, including data-subject rights, lawful-basis requirements, and cross-border transfer constraints.

4. The Privacy Engineer's Manifesto - Michelle Finneran Dennedy, Jonathan Fox & Thomas R. Finneran - Apress - Foundational text on building privacy into systems by design; chapters on PII inventory, retention, and re-identification risk apply directly to xAPI statement design.

5. Data Privacy: A Runbook for Engineers - Nishant Bhajaria - Manning - Practical engineering guidance on PII discovery, pseudonymization, and audit-log design; the case studies match the patterns this chapter recommends for xAPI.

6. [U.S. Department of Education — Student Privacy Policy Office](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/) - U.S. Department of Education - Authoritative US government resource on FERPA, with case studies, FAQs, and model contracts relevant to school-district xAPI deployments.

7. [European Data Protection Board](https://edpb.europa.eu/edpb_en) - EDPB - Official EU guidance on GDPR application, including the binding decisions and guidelines that affect cross-border xAPI data flows.

8. [FTC COPPA Compliance Guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) - U.S. Federal Trade Commission - The FTC's own FAQ on COPPA, including the verifiable-parental-consent methods most relevant to under-13 textbook deployments.

9. [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) - U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology - Voluntary framework for managing privacy risk, with mappings to security controls that an LRS operator can adopt.

10. [Future of Privacy Forum — Education Resources](https://fpf.org/issue/student-privacy/) - Future of Privacy Forum - Industry-and-academia think tank with K-12 and higher-ed privacy guidance specifically focused on edtech deployments.

## Canvas-Height Iteration

The height required two passes — documented because the failure mode is
silent (the iframe has `overflow: hidden` and bottom events disappear
without warning).

| Attempt | Timeline JS height | CANVAS_HEIGHT | Iframe height | Result |
|---------|--------------------|---------------|---------------|--------|
| First   | 320px              | 600           | 602px         | Bottom event "I2IDL TSC" clipped — only 11 of 14 fully visible |
| Second  | 400px              | 680           | 682px         | All 14 events fully visible, plus right-edge fix |

A separate fix was needed for the right-edge clipping of `I2IDL Technical
Steering Committee`: the headline was shortened to `I2IDL TSC`, and the
visible-window padding was extended from 3 years to 6 years on each side.

## What Went Smoothly

- The vis-timeline guide's **edge-clipping CSS fix** (Part 1: `overflow:
  visible !important` on `.vis-timeline`, `.vis-panel.vis-center`, and
  `.vis-item-overflow`; Part 2: `align: 'center'`; Part 3: manual
  `setWindow()` with padding instead of `fit()`) worked first try.
- The **scroll-hijacking fix** (capture-phase wheel listener that
  `stopImmediatePropagation()` on vertical scroll, manual pan on horizontal)
  copied directly from the guide's troubleshooting section.
- `validate-sims.py` gave a precise punch list — five issues, all
  remediable in ~10 lines of edits each.

## What Required Iteration

- **CANVAS_HEIGHT estimation.** The first guess (600) was based on header +
  filter bar + 320px timeline + footer arithmetic; it didn't account for
  vis-timeline's stack height when 14 events overlap in a narrow window.
  Lesson: when many items can overlap, generously over-allocate the
  timeline container height and let `setWindow` padding do the rest.
- **The right-edge `I2IDL Technical Steering Committee` headline.** Even
  after fixing CSS overflow, the text extended past the visible window.
  Two-part fix: shortened the headline to `I2IDL TSC` (full name still
  appears in the infobox) + bumped window padding from 3y to 6y.
- **The user's vis-network → vis-timeline correction.** The initial
  invocation routed to the wrong guide. The pivot cost only the time to
  re-read `timeline-guide.md`; nothing was written before the correction
  arrived.
